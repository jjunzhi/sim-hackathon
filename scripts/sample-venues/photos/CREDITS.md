# SAMPLE room photo credits

These photos stand in for the rooms of SAMPLE (fictional) venues. They do not show the
venues named in the app, which don't exist. Each was downloaded once on 2026-10-22,
cropped and resized to 2048x1536, and had its metadata stripped. Before use, each was
checked by eye for people and logos (notes below).

[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Both allow free commercial use and
storing copies in an app, without attribution; credit is given here anyway. Neither
allows selling unaltered copies or redistributing them as stock.
